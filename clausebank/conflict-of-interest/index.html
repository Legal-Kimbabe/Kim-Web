COI-01利益衝突長版Conflict of Interest顧問、委任、代理、經銷、合作或投資，以及其他可能涉及多重角色、關係企業或競業關係之契約。
任一方如知悉其本人、關係企業、董事、經理人、受僱人、代理人或其他代表，於本契約之履行過程中存在或可能存在任何利益衝突時，應立即以書面通知他方，並揭露足以使他方合理判斷該利益衝突之性質、範圍及可能影響之相關資訊。

未經他方事前書面同意，該方不得從事任何足以實質影響其依本契約履行義務、公正性、獨立性或忠實義務之行為、交易或安排。

如該利益衝突得透過資訊隔離、職務迴避、利益揭露、限制特定人員參與，或其他合理措施予以適當管理，雙方得另以書面約定其處理方式。
If either Party becomes aware of any actual or potential conflict of interest involving itself, its Affiliates, directors, officers, employees, agents, or other representatives in connection with the performance of this Agreement, such Party shall promptly notify the other Party in writing and disclose sufficient information to enable the other Party to reasonably assess the nature, scope, and potential impact of such conflict.

Unless the other Party has given its prior written consent, the affected Party shall not engage in any activity, transaction, or arrangement that may materially impair its ability to perform its obligations under this Agreement with due independence, impartiality, or fidelity.

Where such conflict can reasonably be managed through information barriers, recusal, disclosure, restrictions on participation by certain personnel, or other appropriate safeguards, the Parties may agree in writing on the applicable mitigation measures.
