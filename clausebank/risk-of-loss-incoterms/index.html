披薩口味參考
FOB：[裝運港名稱]裝運港交貨，貨物裝載上船時風險移轉。
DAP：[指定目的地]貨物運抵指定目的地，置於抵達之運輸工具上準備卸貨時風險移轉。
CIF：[指定目的港]賣方負擔運費及保險至指定目的港，風險於裝運港裝船時移轉。
CFR：[指定目的港]同 CIF，但賣方不負擔保險。
FCA：[指定交貨地點]賣方於指定地點將貨物交付予買方指定之承運人或其他指定人時，風險移轉。
DDP：[指定目的地]賣方將貨物運至指定目的地並完成進口通關，貨物於指定目的地準備卸貨時風險移轉。
EXW：[指定交貨地點]賣方於其營業場所或其他指定地點將貨物置於買方處置時，風險移轉。
Pizza Flavor Reference
FOB: [named port of shipment]Risk transfers when the goods are loaded on board the vessel at the port of shipment.
DAP: [named place of destination]Risk transfers when the goods arrive at the named place on the arriving means of transport, ready for unloading.
CIF: [named port of destination]The seller pays freight and insurance to the named port of destination; risk transfers when the goods are loaded on board at the port of shipment.
CFR: [named port of destination]Same risk point as CIF, but the seller does not provide insurance.
FCA: [named place of delivery]Risk transfers when the seller delivers the goods to the carrier or other person nominated by the buyer at the named place.
DDP: [named place of destination]The seller delivers the goods to the named destination after import clearance; risk transfers when the goods are ready for unloading at destination.
EXW: [named place of delivery]Risk transfers when the seller places the goods at the buyer’s disposal at the seller’s premises or another named place.
