TM-04終止效果短版Effects of Termination
本契約終止或屆期時，除本契約另有約定外，雙方應結清截至終止或屆期生效日止已發生之款項及其他債務，並依他方要求返還或銷毀因履行本契約所取得之文件、資料及其他財物。依其性質或本契約約定應於終止或屆期後繼續有效之條款，不因本契約終止或屆期而失其效力。
Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, unless otherwise provided herein, the Parties shall settle all amounts and other obligations accrued up to the effective date of termination or expiration and, upon the other Party’s request, return or destroy any documents, information, and other property obtained in connection with the performance of this Agreement. Any provision which by its nature or by the terms of this Agreement is intended to survive termination or expiration shall remain in full force and effect.
