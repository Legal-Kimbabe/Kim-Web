FM-03展延工期長版Extension of Time工程承攬／不可抗力致工期延誤
一、乙方因不可抗力或其他不可歸責於己之事由，致遲延完成履約進度者，得向甲方申請展延工期。二、前項所稱事由，包括下列由乙方提出相關事證並經甲方核定之情形：1. 因山崩、地震、海嘯、火山爆發、颱風或其他天然災害，致無法履約者；2. 國際情勢發生重大變故，或受戰爭、武裝衝突影響者；3. 國內經濟發生重大變故，或發生叛亂、暴動或罷工者；4. 政府法令之新增或變更；5. 政府機關依法令或行政命令要求停工、徵用、沒入、拆除或禁止運輸者；或6. 其他經甲方認定屬不可歸責於乙方之事由。三、乙方應於遲延事由發生後七（7）日內，以書面通知甲方，並提出相關事證及書面說明，敘明遲延之情形及原因、預計受影響之期間，以及已採取或預計採取之防止或減輕遲延措施，據以申請展延工期。乙方未於前述期限內通知並申請展延者，不得再以同一事由申請展延工期。四、甲方收到乙方之展延申請後，得依該事由對履約進度之實際影響程度及相關事證進行審查，並以書面核定展延期間；經甲方核定者，本契約所定之履約期限或相關預定時程應依核定期間相應展延。五、依本條核准之工期展延，僅生展延履約期限之效力，不因此增加契約價金；乙方不得以工期展延為由向甲方請求任何額外費用或補償。
I. If Party B is delayed in completing the performance schedule due to force majeure or any other cause not attributable to Party B, Party B may apply to Party A for an extension of time.II. Such causes shall include the following circumstances, subject to Party B providing relevant supporting evidence and Party A’s approval:1. landslides, earthquakes, tsunamis, volcanic eruptions, typhoons, or other natural disasters that prevent performance;2. material changes in international circumstances, war, or armed conflict;3. material changes in domestic economic conditions, rebellion, civil unrest, or strikes;4. the enactment of or changes to applicable laws or regulations;5. any suspension of work, requisition, confiscation, demolition, transportation restriction, or similar measure imposed by a governmental authority pursuant to applicable law or administrative order; or6. any other cause determined by Party A to be not attributable to Party B.III. Party B shall, within seven (7) days after the occurrence of the event causing the delay, give Party A written notice thereof and submit relevant supporting evidence and written particulars specifying the circumstances and cause of the delay, the anticipated period affected, and the measures taken or proposed to prevent or mitigate the delay. If Party B fails to give notice and apply for an extension within the foregoing period, Party B shall not thereafter be entitled to seek an extension based on the same event.IV. Upon receipt of Party B’s application, Party A may review the actual impact of the relevant event on the performance schedule and the supporting evidence submitted by Party B and determine in writing the period of extension, if any. Any contractual deadline or applicable milestone shall be extended accordingly to the extent approved by Party A.V. Any extension of time granted under this Section shall operate solely to extend the applicable time for performance and shall not result in any increase in the Contract Price. Party B shall not be entitled to any additional payment or compensation by reason of such extension.
