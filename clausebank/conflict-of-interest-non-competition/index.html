COI-03利益衝突長版Conflict of Interest競業禁止，適合高階經理人、顧問及其他可能同時參與競爭事業之契約。
於本契約有效期間內，未經甲方事前書面同意，乙方不得直接或間接受僱於、受任於、擔任顧問、董事、經理人、代理人，或以其他方式參與與甲方業務具有實質競爭關係之事業。

乙方亦不得從事任何足以實質影響其履行本契約義務，或可能造成其對甲方所負義務與其對競爭事業之職務、利益或責任發生衝突之行為。

如乙方對特定業務、職務或合作是否構成競業或利益衝突有疑義，應事先以書面向甲方揭露並取得其同意。
During the term of this Agreement, without the prior written consent of Party A, Party B shall not, directly or indirectly, be employed by, engaged by, serve as a consultant, director, officer, agent, or otherwise participate in any business that is in material competition with the business of Party A.

Party B shall not engage in any activity that may materially interfere with the performance of its obligations under this Agreement or create a conflict between its duties owed to Party A and any position, interest, or responsibility it holds in relation to a competing business.

If Party B is uncertain whether any proposed business activity, position, or engagement constitutes competition or a conflict of interest, Party B shall disclose the relevant circumstances to Party A in writing in advance and obtain Party A’s consent.
