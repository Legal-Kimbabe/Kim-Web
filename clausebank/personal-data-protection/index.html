PD-01個人資料保護長版Personal Data Protection一般個資蒐集、處理或利用
任一方於履行本契約過程中，如有蒐集、處理或利用個人資料之情形，應依適用之個人資料保護法令及本契約之約定辦理，並僅得於履行本契約所必要之範圍內使用該等個人資料。

任一方應採取合理且適當之安全措施，防止個人資料遭未經授權之存取、使用、洩漏、變更、毀損或滅失。

除法令另有規定或經資料當事人合法同意外，任一方不得將因履行本契約所取得之個人資料，用於與本契約無關之目的，或提供予無權接觸該等資料之第三人。
If either Party collects, processes, or uses any Personal Data in connection with the performance of this Agreement, such Party shall comply with applicable data protection laws and the terms of this Agreement, and shall use such Personal Data only to the extent necessary for the performance of this Agreement.

Each Party shall implement reasonable and appropriate security measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, damage, or loss.

Unless otherwise required by applicable law or validly authorized by the relevant data subject, neither Party shall use any Personal Data obtained in connection with this Agreement for purposes unrelated to this Agreement or disclose such Personal Data to any third party not authorized to access it.
