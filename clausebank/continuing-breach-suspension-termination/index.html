任一方違反本契約之約定，且該違約情事具有持續性者，未違約方得以書面通知要求其於【X日】內改正；於違約情事改正前，未違約方得於合理必要範圍內暫停履行與該違約事項相關之義務。違約方逾期仍未改正者，他方得終止本契約，並得請求因此所受之損害。
Where a party is in continuing breach of this Contract, the non-breaching party may require such breach to be cured within [X days] by written notice and, pending cure, may suspend performance of its obligations relating to the breach to the extent reasonably necessary. If the breach remains uncured upon expiration of the cure period, the non-breaching party may terminate this Contract and recover any damages resulting therefrom.
