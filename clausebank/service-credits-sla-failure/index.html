SLA-03服務水準長版Service Level Agreement違反 SLA 之約定
如乙方於任一計費期間未達雙方約定之服務水準，甲方得依本契約或相關附件之約定，就受影響之服務申請服務抵扣。

除雙方另有書面約定外，服務抵扣得依未達成之服務水準程度，按當期相關服務費用之 [●]% 計算；如同一計費期間內發生多次服務水準未達成情形，累計服務抵扣金額以當期相關服務費用之 [●]% 為上限。

甲方應於知悉服務水準未達成之日起 [●] 日 內提出服務抵扣申請，逾期未提出者，視為放棄該次服務抵扣權利。

除本契約另有約定外，服務抵扣不影響甲方依本契約就重大違約、持續性服務中斷或其他可歸責於乙方之情形主張其他救濟之權利。
If Party B fails to meet any agreed service level during an applicable billing period, Party A may claim service credits with respect to the affected Services in accordance with this Agreement or the applicable schedule.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the service credit may be calculated at [●]% of the applicable service fees for the relevant billing period, depending on the extent of the service level failure. Where multiple service level failures occur during the same billing period, the aggregate service credits shall not exceed [●]% of the applicable service fees for that billing period.

Party A shall submit any claim for service credits within [●] days after becoming aware of the applicable service level failure. Failure to submit a claim within such period shall constitute a waiver of the relevant service credit.

Unless otherwise provided in this Agreement, the availability of service credits shall not prejudice Party A’s right to pursue any other remedies in respect of any material breach, repeated service interruption, or other circumstance attributable to Party B.
