IP-02智慧財產權長版Intellectual Property Rights委託設計／成果歸甲方
1. 乙方因執行本契約乙方工作所生之設計成果（包括但不限於規劃設計圖說、工程圖說、文案、影片及圖片等，以下合稱「本著作」），其著作財產權及其他依法得讓與之智慧財產權均歸甲方所有，甲方享有本著作之一切權利。乙方並應確保本著作之著作人同意不對甲方及甲方指定之人行使著作人格權。本著作非經甲方事前書面同意，乙方不得任意使用；如乙方因業務需求，須將本著作用於自身業績展示說明並取得甲方同意者，不在此限，但應於甲方就本工程對外公開營運後始得使用，且不得侵害甲方權益。2. 乙方保證本著作無違反著作權、智慧財產權等相關法規之情事；如涉及第三人權利，均已取得合法授權，且無侵害他人權益或違反法令之情形。如因此致甲方受有損害，均由乙方負責解決，概與甲方無涉，乙方並應賠償甲方所受之全部損害（包括但不限於訴訟費用及律師費用等）。3. 本條款於本契約屆滿、終止或解除後仍繼續有效。
1. All economic rights and other transferable intellectual property rights in the design works created by Party B in performing its obligations under this Agreement, including but not limited to planning and design drawings, engineering drawings, written content, videos, and images (collectively, the “Works”), shall belong to Party A, and Party A shall have all rights in and to the Works. Party B shall also ensure that the authors of the Works agree not to exercise their moral rights against Party A or any person designated by Party A. Party B shall not use the Works without Party A’s prior written consent; provided that, where Party B obtains Party A’s consent to use the Works for its own portfolio or business presentation purposes, such use may only occur after Party A has publicly commenced operation of the relevant project and shall not prejudice Party A’s rights or interests.2. Party B represents and warrants that the Works do not infringe any copyright or other intellectual property rights or violate any applicable laws and that all necessary licenses have been duly obtained where any third-party rights are involved. If Party A suffers any loss or damage as a result of any such infringement or violation, Party B shall be responsible for resolving the matter and shall indemnify Party A for all losses and damages incurred, including but not limited to litigation costs and attorneys’ fees.3. This provision shall survive the expiration, termination, or rescission of this Agreement.
