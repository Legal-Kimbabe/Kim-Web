AB-02調解與仲裁長版Mediation Followed by Arbitration公部門契約／先調後仲
雙方因本契約所生或與本契約有關之爭議，包括本契約之存續、效力、履行或終止等事項，應於法令及本契約範圍內，考量公共利益之維護及公平合理原則，本於誠信協調解決。如雙方未能經協調達成協議，應將爭議提交中華民國仲裁協會爭議調解中心，依該中心之調解規則於臺北進行調解。如經前述調解程序仍未能解決爭議，該爭議應提交中華民國仲裁協會，依中華民國仲裁法及該協會之仲裁規則於臺北進行仲裁。仲裁庭由三名仲裁人組成，並依前述仲裁規則選定之；仲裁程序應以中文進行，並以中文作成仲裁判斷。仲裁判斷為終局，對雙方均具有拘束力。
Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any dispute concerning its existence, validity, performance, or termination, shall first be addressed through good-faith consultation between the parties in accordance with applicable law and this Agreement, taking into account the public interest and principles of fairness and reasonableness.If the parties are unable to resolve the dispute through consultation, the dispute shall be submitted to the Dispute Mediation Center of the Chinese Arbitration Association, Taipei (CAA), for mediation in Taipei in accordance with its applicable mediation rules.If the dispute remains unresolved following such mediation, it shall be submitted to the Chinese Arbitration Association, Taipei (CAA), for final resolution by arbitration in Taipei in accordance with the Arbitration Act of the Republic of China (Taiwan) and the applicable arbitration rules of the CAA. The arbitral tribunal shall consist of three arbitrators appointed in accordance with such rules. The arbitration shall be conducted in Chinese, and the arbitral award shall be rendered in Chinese. The arbitral award shall be final and binding upon the parties.
