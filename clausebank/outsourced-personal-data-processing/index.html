PD-02個人資料保護長版Personal Data Protection委外處理個人資料
如乙方因履行本契約而受甲方委託蒐集、處理或利用個人資料，乙方僅得依甲方之指示及本契約約定處理該等個人資料，不得逾越受託目的或另作其他利用。

乙方應採取適當之技術及組織安全措施，防止個人資料遭未經授權之存取、使用、洩漏、變更、毀損或滅失，並應確保其受僱人、受任人或其他得接觸個人資料之人員負有相應之保密及資料保護義務。

未經甲方事前書面同意，乙方不得將受託之個人資料再委由第三人處理；如經甲方同意再委託者，乙方應確保該第三人承擔不低於本契約所定之個人資料保護義務。

本契約終止、解除或受託目的完成後，除法令另有規定外，乙方應依甲方指示返還、刪除或銷毀因履行本契約所持有之個人資料及其複本。
Where Party B collects, processes, or uses Personal Data on behalf of Party A in connection with the performance of this Agreement, Party B shall process such Personal Data solely in accordance with Party A’s instructions and the terms of this Agreement, and shall not use such Personal Data beyond the purposes for which it was entrusted.

Party B shall implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, damage, or loss, and shall ensure that its employees, contractors, and other personnel with access to such Personal Data are subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection obligations.

Party B shall not engage any third party to process the entrusted Personal Data without Party A’s prior written consent. Where such engagement is approved, Party B shall ensure that the third party is bound by data protection obligations no less protective than those set forth in this Agreement.

Upon termination or rescission of this Agreement, or completion of the entrusted purpose, Party B shall, unless otherwise required by applicable law, return, delete, or destroy the Personal Data and any copies thereof in accordance with Party A’s instructions.
