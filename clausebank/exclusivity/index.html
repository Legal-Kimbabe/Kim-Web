MI-13獨家合作短版Exclusivity
於本合約有效期間內，甲方授予乙方就本合約約定之合作事項享有獨家合作權。未經乙方事前書面同意，甲方不得自行或委由任何第三人從事與本合約合作事項相同或實質相似之業務。
During the term of this Agreement, Party A grants Party B the exclusive right to engage in the cooperation contemplated under this Agreement. Without Party B’s prior written consent, Party A shall not, directly or through any third party, engage in any business that is the same as or substantially similar to the cooperation contemplated under this Agreement.
