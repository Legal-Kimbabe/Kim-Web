IP-04智慧財產權長版Intellectual Property Rights合作經營／既有權利與履約成果
1. 雙方於本契約簽訂前已擁有或於履約過程中提供予他方之文件、資料、商標、營業秘密、know-how 及其他智慧財產權，仍歸原權利人所有。除本契約另有約定或經權利人事前書面同意外，他方不得於本契約目的以外使用、重製、引用或提供予第三人。2. 乙方之商標、公司名稱及其他商業識別，僅得於履行本契約所必要之範圍內使用。本契約屆滿、終止或乙方不再受託提供服務時，甲方應停止使用之。3. 因乙方執行本契約所產生之客戶原始資料、分析資料及統計資料，其所有權歸甲方所有；但乙方原有之 know-how、商標及其他既有智慧財產權，不因此移轉予甲方。未經甲方事前書面同意，乙方不得將前述屬於甲方之資料提供予第三人或作本契約目的以外之使用。

All documents, information, trademarks, trade secrets, know-how, and other intellectual property owned by either Party prior to this Agreement or provided to the other Party in connection with its performance shall remain the property of the original rights holder. Unless otherwise provided in this Agreement or with the rights holder’s prior written consent, the other Party shall not use, reproduce, incorporate, or disclose such materials to any third party for any purpose outside the scope of this Agreement.2. Party B’s trademarks, company name, and other commercial identifiers may be used solely to the extent necessary for the performance of this Agreement. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, or when Party B ceases to provide the Services, Party A shall cease all such use.3. All raw customer data, analytical data, and statistical data generated by Party B in performing this Agreement shall belong to Party A; provided, however, that Party B’s pre-existing know-how, trademarks, and other intellectual property shall not be transferred to Party A as a result thereof. Without Party A’s prior written consent, Party B shall not disclose any such data belonging to Party A to any third party or use it for any purpose outside the scope of this Agreement.
