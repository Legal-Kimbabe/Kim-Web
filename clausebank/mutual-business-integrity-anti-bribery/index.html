BI-03誠信條款長版Business Integrity and Anti-Bribery雙向拘束版
一、雙方均應以誠信方式進行本契約相關之商業活動，且任一方及其董事、經理人、受僱人、代理人或代表，不得直接或間接向他方之董事、經理人、受僱人、代理人、代表或其他相關人員提供、承諾、要求或給予任何佣金、回扣、酬金、禮品、招待或其他不正當利益，以取得、維持或影響任何交易、合作、商業決定或其他不當利益；惟符合一般商業慣例且屬正當社交禮俗者，不在此限。二、任一方如知悉與本契約有關之人員有違反前項約定之情形，應立即通知他方，並於合理可得之範圍內提供相關資訊及證據，且配合他方進行合理之調查。三、任一方違反本條約定，經他方通知後未於合理期間內改善，或其違反情節重大而無法改善者，他方得立即終止本契約，並得請求因此所受之損害。
I. Each party shall conduct all business activities relating to this Agreement with integrity. Neither party, nor any of its directors, officers, employees, agents, or representatives, shall directly or indirectly offer, promise, request, or provide any commission, kickback, remuneration, gift, entertainment, or other improper benefit to any director, officer, employee, agent, representative, or other personnel of the other party for the purpose of obtaining, retaining, or influencing any transaction, business arrangement, commercial decision, or other improper advantage; provided, however, that customary business courtesies consistent with generally accepted business practices shall not be prohibited.II. If either party becoming aware of any conduct in violation of the foregoing in connection with this Agreement shall promptly notify the other party, provide such relevant information and supporting evidence as is reasonably available, and reasonably cooperate with any investigation conducted by the other party.III. If either party breaches this provision and fails to cure such breach within a reasonable period following notice, or if the breach is material and incapable of cure, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement immediately and recover any damages resulting from such breach.
