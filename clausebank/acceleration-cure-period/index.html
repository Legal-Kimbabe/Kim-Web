如乙方未依本契約約定支付任何到期款項，且經甲方通知後於 [●] 日 內仍未清償，或乙方發生其他重大違約且未於約定之補正期間內完成補正者，甲方得以書面通知乙方，宣布乙方依本契約尚未到期之全部應付款項立即到期。

If Party B fails to pay any amount when due under this Agreement and such failure remains uncured for [●] days after notice from Party A, or if Party B commits any other material breach and fails to cure such breach within the applicable cure period, Party A may, by written notice to Party B, declare all amounts payable by Party B under this Agreement that have not yet become due to be immediately due and payable.

Upon receipt of such notice, all outstanding principal, fees, and other amounts payable by Party B under this Agreement shall be deemed immediately due and payable.
