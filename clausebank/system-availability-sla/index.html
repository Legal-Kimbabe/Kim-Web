SLA-02服務水準長版Service Level Agreement系統可用率
乙方應確保本服務於每一曆月之系統可用率不低於 [99.9]%。

系統可用率之計算方式為：（該月總服務時間－不可用時間）÷ 該月總服務時間 × 100%。

下列期間不計入不可用時間：經事前通知之例行維護、緊急維護、因甲方或第三方系統、設備、網路或其他非可歸責於乙方之事由所造成之中斷，以及不可抗力事件所造成之服務中斷。

乙方如知悉任何可能實質影響系統可用率之異常或中斷，應儘速採取合理措施恢復服務，並於合理期間內通知甲方。
Party B shall ensure that the monthly availability of the Services is not less than [99.9]%.

System Availability shall be calculated as follows: (Total Service Time for the applicable month – Downtime) ÷ Total Service Time for the applicable month × 100%.

Downtime shall exclude scheduled maintenance notified in advance, emergency maintenance, interruptions caused by Party A, third-party systems, equipment, networks, or other causes not attributable to Party B, and interruptions resulting from Force Majeure Events.

If Party B becomes aware of any incident or interruption that may materially affect System Availability, Party B shall promptly take reasonable measures to restore the Services and notify Party A within a reasonable period.
