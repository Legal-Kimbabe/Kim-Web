BR-04違約責任長版Default and Remedies工程承攬、設備安裝等履約內容複雜之契約
一、乙方如違反本契約有關工程期限、施工進度、工程驗收或保固責任之期限約定，每逾期一日，應按工程總價百分之【0.1】計付違約金，並賠償甲方因此所受之損害。甲方得自應付乙方之工程款、履約保證金或保固保證金中直接扣抵前述違約金；如有不足，乙方仍應負清償責任。違約金累計總額以工程總價百分之【十（10）】為上限。二、除本契約另有約定外，乙方如有違反本契約、未依約施作工程、工程品質、效用或功能不符本契約約定、工程發生瑕疵、遲延完工，或其他不完全履行或不履行之情事，甲方得以書面通知乙方修補瑕疵、減少工程價款、解除或終止本契約。三、乙方於拆卸、運輸或安裝過程中，致設備、物資或其他甲方財產毀損或滅失者，應按該財產之新品價格賠償甲方。四、乙方如遲延或未履行本契約之任何義務，致甲方須以訴訟、仲裁或其他方式主張或維護權利者，除應依本契約約定給付違約金外，並應負擔甲方因此所支出之合理必要費用，包括但不限於律師費、訴訟費、仲裁費、鑑定費及調查費。
I. If Party B fails to comply with any deadline under this Agreement relating to the construction schedule, work progress, inspection and acceptance, or warranty obligations, Party B shall pay liquidated damages at the rate of [0.1]% of the total contract price for each day of delay and shall compensate Party A for any resulting losses. Party A may deduct such liquidated damages from any contract payments, performance bond, or warranty bond payable or refundable to Party B. Party B shall remain liable for any deficiency. The aggregate amount of liquidated damages shall not exceed [ten percent (10%)] of the total contract price.II. Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, if Party B breaches this Agreement, fails to perform the work in accordance herewith, delivers work that does not conform to the agreed quality, utility, or functionality, or otherwise performs incompletely, delays completion, or fails to perform any obligation hereunder, Party A may, by written notice, require Party B to remedy the defects, reduce the contract price, or rescind or terminate this Agreement, as applicable.III. If any equipment, materials, or other property of Party A is damaged or lost during dismantling, transportation, or installation due to Party B, Party B shall compensate Party A based on the replacement cost of new property of the same kind.IV. If Party B delays or fails to perform any obligation under this Agreement, thereby requiring Party A to enforce or protect its rights through litigation, arbitration, or other proceedings, Party B shall, in addition to any liquidated damages payable under this Agreement, reimburse Party A for all reasonable and necessary costs incurred in connection therewith, including, without limitation, attorneys’ fees, litigation costs, arbitration costs, appraisal fees, and investigation expenses.
