BR-06違約處理長版Breach and Remedies催告改善、暫停履行及損害賠償
1. 如任何一方（以下簡稱「違約方」）未能履行其在本契約項下的任何義務，另一方（以下簡稱「未違約方」）有權向違約方發出書面通知，說明違約方未能履行其在本契約項下義務之內容。2. 如在發出書面通知後七（7）天內違約方未改善任何違約事項，未違約方可選擇立即暫停本契約直到違約方改善為止；若因此造成未違約方受有損害時，違約方應負一切損害賠償責任。
1. If either Party (the "Defaulting Party") fails to perform any of its obligations under this Agreement, the other Party (the "Non-Defaulting Party") shall be entitled to give written notice to the Defaulting Party specifying the nature of such failure.2. If the Defaulting Party fails to cure any breach identified in such written notice within seven (7) days after the notice is given, the Non-Defaulting Party may elect to immediately suspend performance of this Agreement until the Defaulting Party has cured such breach. If the Non-Defaulting Party suffers any loss or damage as a result thereof, the Defaulting Party shall be liable for all such loss and damage.
